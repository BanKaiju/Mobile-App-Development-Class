About Batman

Batman is one of DC Comics most popular characters. Bruce Wayne witnessed the murder of his parents as a child, which led him to dedicate his life to fighting crime. During the day, Bruce Wayne is a playboy philanthropist, but at night he becomes Batman and protects Gotham City from the criminal element.

The Dark Knight

Unlike most superheroes, Batman has no superpowers. Instead he must rely on his intelligence, detective skills, physical training, technology, and overall willpower to fight crime.
